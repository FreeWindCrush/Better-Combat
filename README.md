## 📖 What Is Better Combat?

**Better Combat** is a combat overhaul mod for Minecraft Java Edition that brings fluid, animation-driven melee combat to the game. It's inspired by *Minecraft Dungeons* and developed by **daedelus_dev** (ZsoltMolnarrr).

The mod doesn't just add new weapons — it changes *how* you fight. Weapons have visible swing arcs. Attacks have wind-up and recovery phases. Collision detection follows the actual blade path instead of your crosshair. The result is combat that feels more physical, more readable, and more fun.

**Key highlights:**
- ✅ **Dungeons-style animations** — one-handed, two-handed, and dual-wield swings
- ✅ **Accurate hitboxes** — hit what your blade touches, not what your cursor points at
- ✅ **Weapon combos** — different attack sequences per weapon type
- ✅ **Dual-wield any pair** — use two one-handed weapons simultaneously
- ✅ **Hold-to-attack** — no more mouse spamming
- ✅ **JSON API** — add custom weapon behavior via data files
- ✅ **Auto-compatibility** — most modded weapons work out of the box
